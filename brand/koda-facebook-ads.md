# KODA — Facebook Ads creative kit (USP-loaded)

**Goal:** stop the scroll, hit the nerve (fake screenshots = stolen money), then
*prove* KODA is different with KODA's real, unfair advantages — so a mobile-money
merchant reaches for their phone and signs up free, in seconds.

**Why this version:** emotion gets the scroll to stop; **specificity gets the card
out.** Each image below is anchored to ONE true differentiator a competitor
literally cannot claim. Together they form an objection-demolition funnel:

| # | Beat | USP weaponized | Objection it kills |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pain | Loss to fake screenshots | "This isn't my problem" |
| 2 | Effortless | **The SMS is the API — zero telco contract, nothing to install, free until you get paid** | "Sounds complicated / expensive" |
| 3 | Can't be fooled | **Balance-chain firewall — built to be lied to; spoofed SMS is quarantined** | "Will it actually stop fraud?" |
| 4 | Hard proof | **Replay lock — a code used once is dead forever, across all 5 doors** | "They'll just reuse a receipt" |
| 5 | It's for me | **5 doors / any phone — app, WhatsApp, API, USSD, SMS; works on a $15 feature phone, no internet** | "I/my customers don't have the tech" |

### How to use these 5 image prompts
1. Paste each into your image tool (Midjourney, Flux, Ideogram, GPT-image…).
2. **Generate CLEAN (no baked-in text)** — AI garbles words. Each prompt leaves negative space for the headline.
3. Add the **overlay text** (given per image) afterwards in Canva, inside the safe zones, in KODA colours.
4. Palette: deep pine `#081813` / `#0C231C`, gold `#E8A11F`, verify-green `#23B884`, cream `#E9E4D5`, sage `#9BA79B`.
5. Midjourney: append `--ar 4:5 --style raw --v 6` (use `--ar 9:16` for the Stories cut).
6. Specs: 4:5 feed = 1080×1350 (or 1440×1800 retina); keep **top 14% / bottom 20% clear** of text.

**True facts you may put in copy (all verifiable on the product):** ~3-second
verdict · 235 operators / 95 countries · no telco API · single-use replay lock ·
balance-chain anti-forgery · 5 ways to verify · works offline on any phone ·
reads only the payment SMS, never your contacts, never your money · free to start,
no card. **Never claim** bank/wallet/escrow, that it holds/moves money, or any
"guaranteed / 100% / unhackable / proof-of-settlement." KODA makes you **first to know.**

> up right now.
>

## Launch notes
- **Pair each image with a different primary text** (dynamic creative / Advantage+) and let Meta find the winner. Don't guess — test, then scale the winner.
- **Sequence for retargeting:** 1 (pain) → 2 (effortless) → 3/4 (can't-be-fooled / replay) → 5 (any phone). Cold traffic responds best to 1, 2 and 5; warm traffic converts on 3 and 4.
- **Audience:** merchants, retail, small-business owners, mobile-money users; start DRC + live-rail countries (KE/TZ/UG/GH/CI/SN/CM/NG/RW), then widen.
- **Destination:** `https://kodajnn.com/get-started`. The site's first-visit modal echoes the same hook.
- **Offer honesty:** promise only "free to start / free until you get paid" — both true. A real, honoured deadline can lift urgency; a fake one gets flagged by Meta and breaks trust.
- **French-first for DRC**, English for pan-African + developer/AI reach.
